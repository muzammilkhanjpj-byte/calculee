Important Scientific Notice

This computational tool is intended solely for academic, educational, and preliminary in vitro laboratory research reference. It is not an automated dispensing device or clinical medical instrument.

1. Research & Educational Reference Use Only

The Tocris Dilution Calculator is provided as an open-access reference calculation utility for scientific research personnel, chemists, biochemists, and educators. All calculations—including single dilution volumes ($C_1V_1 = C_2V_2$), dry powder mass reconstitutions, and multi-step serial dilution tables—are intended strictly to assist in manual laboratory arithmetic and experimental design.

2. No Medical, Diagnostic, or Clinical Guidance

Under no circumstances should the outputs, protocols, or calculations provided by this application be interpreted as, or used for:

  • Clinical diagnostic determinations or patient treatment plans;
  • Human, veterinary, or clinical drug formulation, administration, or dosing;
  • Therapeutic prescribing or medical decision-making;
  • Manufacturing of regulated pharmaceuticals, vaccines, or medical devices.

3. Researcher Duty of Verification & Quality Assurance

The individual laboratory researcher, principal investigator (PI), or institution remains solely and exclusively responsible for verifying all calculations prior to bench execution. Specifically, users must independently validate:

  • Certificate of Analysis (CoA): Confirm certified molecular weights ($MW$), purity percentages, salt fractions, and hydration states stated on the specific batch data sheet.
  • Solubility Limits: Confirm the thermodynamic solubility of the target solute in the chosen solvent (e.g., DMSO, ethanol, sterile water, or physiological saline) at working temperatures.
  • Vehicle Cytotoxicity Thresholds: Ensure organic carrier solvents (e.g., DMSO) are diluted to safe experimental levels (typically $\le 0.1\%$ v/v in cell culture) to prevent membrane permeability artifacts or cytotoxic cell death.
  • Chemical Reactivity & Safety: Verify safety precautions (e.g., always adding concentrated acids to water to dissipate exothermic hydration heat).

This calculator operates as an independent scientific tool. Reference to commercial product formats, standard vendor terminology, or chemical naming conventions is made strictly for descriptive and scientific educational purposes.
